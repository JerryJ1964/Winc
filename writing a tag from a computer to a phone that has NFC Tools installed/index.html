How to use:
1. Ensure your phone has NFC Tools app installed and running
2. Enable Bluetooth on both computer and phone
3. Click "Scan for Device" below
4. Select your phone from the list
5. Enter the starting 5-digit number
6. Click "Write Tags" to begin
Note: The app uses Serial Port Profile (SPP) UUID. If connection fails, you may need to check your NFC Tools app's Bluetooth UUID.
